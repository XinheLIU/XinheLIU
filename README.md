# Hi, I'm Xinhe (Charles)

Last updated: 2026-10-03

**Agentic System Builder & Designer**

I design and build systems where AI agents and people work together — from coding workflows and shared memory to knowledge tools and personal assistants. My focus is on how the pieces fit: what agents need to know, how they coordinate, how their work is evaluated, and where human judgment belongs.

My earlier experience spans **recommendation and advertising systems, experimentation, and causal measurement**. That background shapes how I approach agentic systems: start with a clear problem, make the tradeoffs explicit, and test whether the system improves the outcome that matters.

I also write to deepen and share what I learn. My essays, technical notes, and books cover AI, software engineering, machine learning, and computer science, often in both English and Chinese. Across building and writing, I aim to make complex ideas understandable and useful.
